Privacy Screenshot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Privacy Screenshot Blur & More helps you hide sensitive details in screenshots on your Android device — blur, pixelate, cover with solid colour, crop, highlight, and share safely. We designed it to work offline and to keep your images on your phone.

What we collect

We do not collect, upload, or sell your personal data.

  • Screenshots you open and edits you make stay on your device unless you choose to share or save them elsewhere.
  • Exported images are re-encoded as new PNG files without original photo metadata (such as EXIF location or camera tags).
  • No account, email, or login is required.
  • No analytics SDKs in the current release.

Permissions

  • Photos / gallery — only when you open an image from your gallery or receive one through Android’s Share menu.
  • Storage — only when you choose Download or Save to store your protected screenshot on your device.

The app does not request camera access. You bring images into the app via the system picker or Share sheet.

Third-party services

Current version: No ads, no cloud upload, and no third-party analytics. All editing runs on your device.

Future updates: If we add optional ads or online features, we will update this page before they ship.

Children's privacy

Privacy Screenshot does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We will update this page if our data practices change.
