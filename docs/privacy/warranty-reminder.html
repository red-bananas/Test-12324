Waro: Warranty & Service Track — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Waro helps you track product warranties, service dates, and supporting documents on your Android device. Your records stay on your phone. We do not sell your personal data.

What we collect

We do not collect, upload, or store your warranty records on developer-operated servers.

Waro has no account system and no developer-operated backend.

  • Product details you enter (name, brand, model, category, purchase and warranty dates) are stored locally in an on-device database.
  • Service history (dates, notes, costs, provider, and related details) is stored locally on your device.
  • Photos and documents you attach are copied into private app storage on your device.
  • Your display name and currency preference may be stored locally for app settings.
  • Reminder schedules are stored locally and delivered through Android notifications.
  • No email, login, or cloud sync is required.
  • We do not use a separate developer analytics SDK in the current release.

Deleting a product removes its warranty, service records, and saved attachments from your device. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Android backup is disabled for this app.

Advertising and consent

The app may use Google AdMob to show banner, native, and interstitial ads when advertising is enabled in your build. AdMob and related Google services may collect and process information such as device identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions, and diagnostics according to your consent choices and Google's Privacy Policy.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents privacy and consent choices before personalized ads are requested. You can review ad privacy choices from Settings when available.

Your warranty records, service records, and saved documents are not sent to AdMob.

Network use

Core warranty and service tracking does not require the internet. Internet access is used for advertising and consent management when ads are enabled.

Permissions

  • Notifications — to remind you about upcoming warranty expiry or service dates when you enable reminders.
  • Photos / gallery — only when you choose images through Android's system photo picker.
  • Camera — only when you choose to capture a photo for a product or document through the system camera flow.
  • Internet and network state — for ads and consent management when advertising is enabled.
  • Boot completed — to reschedule local reminders after device restart or app update.

Third-party services

Current version: Warranty and service data remain on your device. Ads, when enabled, are served through Google AdMob. Consent is managed through Google's User Messaging Platform where applicable.

Future updates: If we add cloud backup, accounts, or other online features, we will update this page before they ship.

Children's privacy

Waro does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We will update this page if our data practices change.
