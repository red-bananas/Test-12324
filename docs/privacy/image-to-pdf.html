Image to PDF — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Image to PDF helps you turn photos into PDF files on your Android device. We designed it to work offline and to keep your files on your phone.

What we collect

We do not collect, upload, or sell your personal data.

  • Photos and PDFs you create stay on your device unless you choose to share or save them elsewhere.
  • Recent PDF names and file paths are stored locally so you can open exports again.
  • No account, email, or login is required.
  • No analytics SDKs in the current release.

Permissions

  • Camera — only when you tap Camera to scan pages for a PDF.
  • Photos / gallery — only when you tap Gallery to pick images.
  • Storage — to save exported PDFs and show them in your recents list.

Third-party services

Current version: No ads, no cloud upload, and no third-party analytics. PDF creation runs on your device.

Future updates: If we add optional ads or online features, we will update this page before they ship.

Children's privacy

Image to PDF does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We will update this page if our data practices change.
