Image to PDF – Scanner & More — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Image to PDF – Scanner & More helps you turn photos, scans, and screenshots into PDF files on your Android device. PDF creation runs on your phone. We do not upload your images for conversion. We do not sell your personal data.

What we collect

We do not collect, upload, or store your images or PDFs on developer-operated servers.

Image to PDF – Scanner & More has no developer-operated backend.

  • Images you select and PDFs you create stay on your device unless you choose to share or save them elsewhere.
  • Recent PDF names and local file references may be stored on your device so you can open exports again.
  • No account, email, or login is required.
  • We do not use a separate developer analytics SDK in the current release.

Advertising and consent

The app uses Google AdMob to show native and interstitial ads. AdMob and related Google services may collect and process information such as device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostics according to your consent choices and Google's Privacy Policy.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents privacy and consent choices before personalized ads are requested. You can review or change ad privacy choices from Settings in the app when available.

Network use

PDF conversion does not require the internet. Internet access is used for advertising, consent management, and related ad network requests.

Permissions

  • Camera — only when you tap Camera to scan pages for a PDF.
  • Photos / gallery — only when you pick images through Android's system photo picker or receive images via Share.
  • Storage / save — only when you choose to save or export a PDF through Android's file picker.
  • Internet and network state — for ads and consent management.

The app does not request broad access to your entire photo library. You choose each image explicitly.

Third-party services

Current version: PDF creation runs on your device. Ads are served through Google AdMob. Consent is managed through Google's User Messaging Platform where applicable.

Future updates: If we add cloud features or other online services, we will update this page before they ship.

Children's privacy

Image to PDF – Scanner & More does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We will update this page if our data practices change.
