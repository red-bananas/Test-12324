Date Alarm — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Date Alarm helps you schedule dependable alarms on future dates and flexible recurrences on your Android device. Core alarms work offline with no account. We do not collect, upload, or sell your personal data in the current release.

What we collect

We do not collect, upload to our servers, or sell your personal data.

Date Alarm has no developer-operated backend in v1.

  • Alarm schedules (labels, dates, times, recurrence rules, sound choices, snooze settings) are stored locally in an on-device database.
  • Alarm ring history (time and outcome) is stored locally on your device.
  • App preferences such as time-format choice are stored locally.
  • A small direct-boot copy of upcoming alarms may be stored so alarms can reschedule after reboot before you unlock your phone.
  • No account, email, or login is required.
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs in the current release.

Network use

Core alarms do not require the internet. Date Alarm does not request network access for scheduling, ringing, or managing your alarms in the current release.

Permissions

  • Exact alarms — to ring at the date and time you set.
  • Notifications — to show ringing alarms, snooze, and dismiss actions.
  • Full-screen intent — to show the lock-screen alarm screen when an alarm rings.
  • Foreground service — to play alarm sound and vibration while the ringing notification is active.
  • Boot completed — to reschedule alarms after device restart or app update.
  • Wake lock and vibration — so alarms can ring reliably.
  • Set alarm — to respond to Android alarm intents and assistant voice commands that create alarms in the app.

Date Alarm does not request calendar, location, contacts, or broad storage permissions in v1.

Third-party services

Current version: No ads, no cloud sync, and no third-party analytics. Alarm scheduling and ringing run on your device.

Future updates: If we add optional ads, cloud backup, or other online features, we will update this page before they ship.

Children's privacy

Date Alarm does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We will update this page if our data practices change.
