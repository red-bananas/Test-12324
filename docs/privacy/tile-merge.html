Merge Tiles — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Merge Tiles: Offline Puzzle is an offline puzzle game. We designed it to collect as little data as possible.

What we collect

Nothing leaves your device by default.

  • Game scores, settings, and in-progress boards are stored locally on your phone using device storage.
  • No account, email, or login is required.

Third-party services

Phase 1 (current): No analytics, ads, or third-party SDKs.

Phase 2 (future, optional): We may add Google AdMob for opt-in rewarded ads and occasional interstitials after game over. AdMob may collect device identifiers per Google's privacy policy. A “Remove ads” in-app purchase will disable interstitials.

Children's privacy

Merge Tiles does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

We will update this page before enabling ads or any online feature.

Contact

Open an issue on the Auto-App repository or contact the developer through the app store listing.
