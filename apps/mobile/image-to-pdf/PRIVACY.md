# Privacy Policy — Image to PDF

**Last updated:** 2026-08-10

Image to PDF processes your photos **entirely on your device**. We do not collect, upload, or sell your images or personal data.

## Play Store URL (use this in Google Play Console)

```
https://red-bananas.github.io/Test-12324/privacy/image-to-pdf.html
```

Do **not** use GitHub `blob/` links — Play's crawler often returns 404 for them.

Published HTML: [docs/privacy/image-to-pdf.html](../../../docs/privacy/image-to-pdf.html) (deployed via [`.github/workflows/pages.yml`](../../../.github/workflows/pages.yml) on push to `main`).

## Data we collect

None. The app works offline and does not use analytics or accounts in v1.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — to capture pages for your PDF (only when you tap Camera).
- **Photos** — to pick images from your gallery (only when you tap Gallery).
- **Storage** — to save exported PDFs locally.

## Third-party services

Phase 1: no ads, no cloud services. Phase 2 ads only after human approval — policy page will be updated first.
