# Privacy Policy — Breathing Guide: Calm & Focus

**Last updated:** 2026-08-29

Breathing Guide is designed to work **entirely offline**. Version 1 does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or usage data.

## Play Store URL (use this in Google Play Console)

```
https://red-bananas.github.io/Test-12324/privacy/breath-cue.html
```

Do **not** use GitHub `blob/` links — Play's crawler often returns 404 for them.

Published HTML: [docs/privacy/breath-cue.html](../../../docs/privacy/breath-cue.html) (deployed via [`.github/workflows/pages.yml`](../../../.github/workflows/pages.yml) on push to `main`).

## Data we collect

None. No account, analytics, advertising, crash-reporting SDK, or backend. No internet permission in v1.

Locally stored on device only:

- App settings (sound, haptics, display preferences)
- User-created breathing patterns
- Safety-notice acknowledgement

Does **not** store breathing-session history or health information.

## Permissions

Notifications, foreground service (media playback), vibration, and wake lock — only for user-started breathing sessions with tones and haptics.

## Third-party services

Phase 1: no ads, no cloud, no analytics SDK.
